The Art of Timekeeping

Engineering Meets Art

Every Alberto watch is a result of the perfect harmony between cutting-edge engineering and centuries-old watchmaking traditions. Our commitment to precision ensures that every movement, component and detail works together to deliver reliability, accuracy and timeless beauty.

Watch Movements

Four Movements. One Vision.

Different movements. Same dedication to excellence.

Mechanical Watches

Powered by a wound mainspring, mechanical watches are the purest form of traditional watchmaking.

Key Characteristics

  • Hand-wound
  • No battery required
  • Classic craftsmanship

Automatic Watches

Uses the motion of your wrist to wind the mainspring, combining convenience with tradition.

Key Characteristics

  • Self-winding
  • Motion powered
  • Smooth and continuous

Quartz Watches

Powered by a battery, quartz watches use a crystal oscillator to keep time with high accuracy.

Key Characteristics

  • High accuracy
  • Low maintenance
  • Battery powered

Smart Watches

Combines modern technology with features like fitness tracking and notifications.

Key Characteristics

  • Health and fitness tracking
  • Smart notifications
  • Connected lifestyle

Inside a Watch

The Components

Every component plays a vital role in keeping time. The exact parts vary by movement type. Here are some of the key components used in mechanical and quartz timekeeping.

Gear Train

Transfers energy and controls motion.

Escapement

Regulates the flow of energy.

Balance Wheel

Helps maintain precise timekeeping.

Mainspring

Stores energy to power the movement.

Quartz Oscillator

Provides accurate timekeeping in quartz movements.
