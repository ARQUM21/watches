PRESERVE YOUR TIMEPIECE

Care for Every Second

Simple care habits can help protect your watch and keep it performing as intended.

  • Follow the care instructions for your model.
  • Keep the case and strap clean using suitable methods.
  • Avoid strong impacts and extreme temperatures.
  • Check water-resistance guidance before exposure.
  • Arrange professional servicing when recommended.
